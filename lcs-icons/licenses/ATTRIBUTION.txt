Icons: Identify the specific replacement filenames covered by this license.

Modifications
-------------
SVG files were prepared for application use by:

- Removing fixed width and height attributes
- Removing editor metadata and unused identifiers
- Normalizing supported black fills to currentColor
- Preserving viewBox, paths, circles, fill rules, and clip rules
- Embedding sanitized SVG markup in lcs-icons.js

The original license documents and download records are retained in the
licenses directory.
